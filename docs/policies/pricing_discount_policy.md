# Pricing & Discount Policy (SYNTHETIC / DEMO)

*Fictional internal policy written for the SmartHealth Consumer Analytics demo dataset.*

## 1. Discount bands
- Premium segment: 0-6%
- Regular segment: 4-10%
- New customers: 8-14% (acquisition allowance)
- Enterprise / bulk: 10-16%, subject to a volume commitment
- The marketplace channel carries an additional 3% funded promotion allowance.

## 2. Festive periods
- October and November festive campaigns permit an incremental 6 percentage points of
  discount across all segments.
- Outside festive windows, any month-on-month movement in average realised discount above
  **2 percentage points** must be explained in the monthly commercial review.

## 3. Price change governance
- List price changes require category manager approval and 30 days notice to channel
  partners.
- Realised price per unit (revenue / units) is the reported price metric, because it
  absorbs both discount and mix effects.

## 4. Interpretation guidance
- A fall in average order value with a stable discount rate is a **mix** effect, not a
  pricing action, and should be explained by naming which products stopped selling.
