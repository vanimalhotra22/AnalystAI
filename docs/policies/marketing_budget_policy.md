# Marketing Budget Policy (SYNTHETIC / DEMO)

*Fictional internal policy written for the SmartHealth Consumer Analytics demo dataset.*

## 1. Budget floors
- Category marketing spend should stay within **+/- 20%** of the trailing three-month
  average unless a reallocation is formally approved.
- A reduction above 25% for any region-category combination requires sign-off from the
  regional commercial head, and must be recorded with an expected demand impact.

## 2. Expected demand response
- Planning uses a short-run spend elasticity of approximately **0.15 to 0.25**: a 30%
  spend reduction is expected to reduce category demand by roughly 4-8% within the same
  month, with a smaller lagged effect in the following month.
- Elasticity assumptions are re-estimated each quarter from campaign history.

## 3. Reallocation discipline
- Budget moved out of a region must be accompanied by a stated payback expectation in the
  receiving region.
- Reallocations are reviewed in the monthly performance meeting alongside revenue variance
  by region.

## 4. Reporting
- Spend, impressions, clicks and conversions are reported per product, region and channel
  each month.
- Any month where spend falls more than 25% and revenue falls more than 10% in the same
  region must be reviewed jointly by marketing and supply chain, because supply
  constraints and demand constraints are frequently confounded.
